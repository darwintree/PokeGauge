# Usage Tips mute for the local calendar day and redraw on enter

Usage Tips are a maintained catalog of titled messages. Entering the results surface picks one uniformly at random. Closing does not hide a single identity forever and does not sticky-pick for the calendar day: it mutes every Usage Tip until the viewer's local calendar date changes. Refresh and a new Matchup are enters; Track edits and the mobile setup/results tab switch are not. An open page does not revive a tip at midnight. Matchup landing, load-error, and invalid-share screens do not show Usage Tips.
