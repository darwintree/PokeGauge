<!-- changelog:en -->
Choose usage sources and rules directly in Settings or from a compact panel in the Pokémon and move pickers. The compact panel closes after a rule is selected; additional rules remain available to expand. Pokémon rankings and recommended moves, items, abilities, and natures load more reliably, with loading status and retry options. Fixed missing move recommendations for some Mega Pokémon and recommendations not updating after a refresh. Switching Pikalytics data months now uses the corresponding ranking cache.
